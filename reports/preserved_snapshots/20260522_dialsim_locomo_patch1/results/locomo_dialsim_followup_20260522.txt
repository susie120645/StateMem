4. Bottom line

Keep:
- LOCOMO tightened verifier gating
  - it removes the unstable fact-answer rewrites
  - it gives the best conv-48 result among the three tested LOCOMO variants

Do not claim:
- that answer-level override itself is the source of the LOCOMO gain
- that the new LOCOMO patch improves DialSim

Safe claim:
- The LOCOMO follow-up validates a shape-constrained verifier policy:
  - Unknown recovery is preserved
  - open-ended fact-answer rewrites are blocked
  - conv-48 becomes more stable and scores best among tested LOCOMO variants

Recommended next step:
- If continuing on LOCOMO, add a separate agreement gate for extractive shapes only, instead of reopening fact_lookup rewrites.
- If continuing on DialSim, keep using contrastive_agreement_full as the main result line and treat this rerun only as a stability check.
